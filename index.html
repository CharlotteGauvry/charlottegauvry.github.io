Visiting Senior Fellow, Department of Philosophy — King's College London

Alexander von Humboldt Foundation, Feodor Lynen Fellow

About

I am a philosopher working on the philosophy of consciousness. My research sits at the intersection of the philosophy of mind and the history of philosophy, and draws on empirical and clinical evidence to test philosophical claims about phenomenality.

Research

My primary work is on the metaphysics of consciousness — questions of phenomenal unity, higher-order representationalism, and illusionism — approached in part through borderline cases of consciousness, including depersonalisation disorders and hemispherotomy.

I also work in the history of philosophy, particularly early phenomenology, Wittgensteinian studies and German philosophy.

  • Phenomenal unity
  • Higher-order representationalism
